STEAL AN ITEM

63 levels. Every cop is a different creature and gets faster each level. Clear level 63 to win. Lobby: you + 4 bots (5 max).

1. Train: step on the treadmill in your yard and tap E once — it runs for you. Tap E again (or move) to stop. Upgrade it with U ; it evolves from budget to galaxy.
2. Steal: press E next to an item. Gates need more speed as you go.
3. Escape: the item becomes a mystery sack and a cop chases you. Get it into your fenced yard and press E to drop it.
4. Wait & open: when the timer finishes, press E beside the sack. Opened items go in your bag: press F in your yard to place one anywhere you like (F beside it again to pick it up). Placed items earn cash every second. A day lasts 6 minutes; the 15-second night makes sacks open 2× faster, and stolen items only respawn at dawn. The shop sells 20 trails. Visit the shop (right of your house) for pets and outfits. At the green sign press P to upgrade your plot: every level adds an item slot and a sack slot.
